# Raven's Hollow — Concept v001

Status:
Inspirational concept.

- Warm light against a dark world.
- Naturally grown village.
- Stream defines settlement.
- Wilderness begins immediately beyond safety.
- Designed to feel like home, not a tutorial.

Not canon:

- Scale.
- Architecture.

This image exists to preserve feeling, not geometry.
