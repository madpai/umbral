# Foundational Laws of Reality

> These laws are the deepest truths presently known about the universe of UMBRAL.

They are not mythology.

They are not religion.

They are not scientific theories.

They are the immutable principles upon which every future cosmological, historical, cultural, technological, and supernatural system must be built.

Future documents may expand understanding of these laws.

They may never contradict them.

---

# The First Truth

Reality accumulates.

Existence is not passive.

Every action changes the world.

Every interaction leaves an imprint.

Every relationship alters reality.

Nothing participates without consequence.

Some changes disappear almost immediately.

Some survive for generations.

Some become history itself.

The universe remembers through accumulation.

---

# Resonance

The property through which reality preserves participation is known as Resonance.

Resonance is not magic.

It is not energy.

It is not a resource.

It is a fundamental characteristic of existence.

Everything that exists participates in Resonance.

Everything that participates leaves impressions.

Some impressions fade.

Some strengthen.

Some become so deeply embedded that they influence the future.

Resonance explains why places develop identity, why artifacts acquire significance, why traditions endure, and why history matters.

It is the foundation from which all extraordinary phenomena must ultimately emerge.

---

# The Three Laws of Resonance

## First Law

Nothing leaves the world unchanged.

Every interaction produces an imprint upon reality.

Whether that imprint lasts for a heartbeat or a thousand years depends upon what follows.

---

## Second Law

Repetition strengthens Resonance.

Repeated participation deepens reality.

One traveler leaves footprints.

Thousands create a road.

One family occupies a house.

Generations create a home.

One battle stains the earth.

Centuries of conflict reshape an entire frontier.

Resonance grows through continuity.

---

## Third Law

Resonance cannot exist without participation.

Nothing meaningful appears without history.

Power cannot emerge without relationship.

No object, place, institution, tradition, or individual acquires significance in isolation.

Everything must earn its influence through existence.

---

# The Umbral

The visible world is not the entirety of reality.

Beneath ordinary perception exists the deeper structure through which Resonance accumulates.

This hidden structure is known as the Umbral.

The Umbral is not another world.

It is not an afterlife.

It is not a parallel dimension.

It is the underlying fabric through which consequence persists.

Most people experience only its effects.

Few understand it.

No known civilization has ever fully explained it.

Every culture interprets it differently.

Whether through religion, philosophy, scholarship, superstition, or lived experience, all attempts to understand extraordinary phenomena are ultimately attempts to understand the Umbral.

---

# Consequence

Because Resonance accumulates, consequence is fundamental.

Nothing can simply return reality to a previous state.

Damage may heal.

Buildings may be rebuilt.

Relationships may recover.

Knowledge may be rediscovered.

Yet history itself remains part of the world.

The universe does not forget.

---

# Permanence and Change

Persistence does not require permanence.

Some things should disappear.

Some things should decay.

Some things should be forgotten.

Some things should endure.

The universe constantly balances preservation and transformation.

Resonance preserves meaning rather than preventing change.

---

# Extraordinary Phenomena

Every phenomenon that appears supernatural must ultimately obey these laws.

Different cultures may explain the same phenomenon differently.

Their explanations may conflict.

Their beliefs may be incomplete.

Reality itself remains internally consistent.

There are not multiple incompatible systems governing miracles, curses, artifacts, gods, monsters, or unexplained events.

They are all expressions of the same universe.

---

# Design Constraint

These laws are considered canonical.

Future documents must remain compatible with them.

Future discoveries may reveal that existing cultures misunderstand these laws.

The laws themselves do not change.
