# How We Know

> Reality is constant. Understanding is not.

This document explains how knowledge exists within the universe of UMBRAL.

It is not a history of civilizations.

It is not cosmology.

It defines how truth is discovered, preserved, distorted, forgotten, and rediscovered.

Every future culture, religion, institution, philosophy, and scholarly tradition must remain compatible with these principles.

---

# Reality Exists Independent of Belief

Reality does not require belief in order to exist.

Resonance functions whether anyone understands it.

The Umbral exists whether anyone can perceive it.

Truth is not created by consensus.

Civilizations discover reality.

They do not define it.

---

# Discovery Rather Than Revelation

No civilization has ever possessed complete understanding.

There was no first culture that learned every truth.

There was no golden age of perfect knowledge.

There was no universal revelation.

Understanding grows gradually through observation, participation, experimentation, preservation, and experience.

Knowledge accumulates in the same way that Resonance accumulates.

Slowly.

Imperfectly.

Across generations.

---

# Every Culture Is Partially Correct

Every culture observes genuine aspects of reality.

Every culture also misunderstands some portion of it.

Religions.

Builders.

Artists.

Farmers.

Historians.

Engineers.

Explorers.

Philosophers.

Each studies reality through different experiences.

Each develops language suited to those experiences.

No single perspective explains everything.

Truth emerges through many incomplete perspectives rather than one perfect authority.

---

# Knowledge Has Consequences

Ideas change civilizations.

A new method of construction changes cities.

A forgotten agricultural practice changes kingdoms.

A misunderstood historical event changes politics.

Knowledge is one of the most powerful forms of influence because understanding changes behavior.

The pursuit of truth therefore carries responsibility.

---

# Memory Is Fragile

Knowledge can be preserved.

Knowledge can also disappear.

Libraries burn.

Languages evolve.

Traditions fracture.

Institutions collapse.

Witnesses die.

Records decay.

Interpretations drift.

No discovery is permanently secure.

Civilizations must actively preserve understanding or risk losing it.

---

# Evidence Exists

Reality leaves evidence.

Places retain history.

Objects retain participation.

Landscapes reveal consequence.

Patterns emerge through careful observation.

Not every claim deserves equal confidence.

Evidence matters.

Interpretation remains difficult.

The challenge is rarely finding information.

The challenge is understanding it correctly.

---

# Expertise Is Valuable

Experience creates insight.

Observation creates understanding.

Mastery deserves respect.

However...

No individual possesses complete truth.

Experts illuminate reality.

They do not own it.

Every scholar, priest, artisan, explorer, and philosopher has blind spots.

Expertise should increase confidence without eliminating humility.

---

# Mystery Is Permanent

Some mysteries exist because knowledge has been lost.

Some because evidence is incomplete.

Some because observation is difficult.

Some because no one has yet asked the correct question.

Mystery is not a temporary absence of exposition.

It is a natural consequence of finite understanding within an ancient universe.

---

# Discovery Is Participation

Learning changes both the learner and the world.

Knowledge is not separate from participation.

Every discovery becomes part of history.

Every interpretation influences future generations.

Understanding itself contributes to Resonance.

The pursuit of truth therefore becomes another way in which people leave something behind.

---

# Creative Constraint

Future cultures may disagree profoundly.

Their disagreements should emerge from differing experiences rather than arbitrary contradiction.

No future document should establish one civilization as possessing complete knowledge of reality.

Every worldview should illuminate one portion of truth while remaining incomplete.

This is intentional.

It is fundamental to UMBRAL.
