# World Overview

This document establishes the philosophical rules of the world of UMBRAL. It
does not define specific lore, names, mechanics, or content.

## A Living World

The world exists independently of any individual player. Players discover it
and become part of it; they do not create it from nothing.

## Continuity

The world has a durable internal identity. Its past remains meaningful, its
changes should be understandable, and its future should emerge from
established foundations. The world should feel older than any individual
player and continue beyond any single session.

## Coherence

The world should be ancient, interconnected, and internally consistent.
History should influence geography, culture should influence behavior, and
the environment should be able to communicate meaning without requiring
explicit narration.

## Inhabitants and Agency

Players are inhabitants rather than automatically designated heroes. Their
identity is earned through actions, relationships, and reputation. The world
should provide room for players to create their own stories while preserving
its own identity and purpose.

## Discovery

Knowledge is valuable. The world should reward curiosity, observation,
experimentation, and learning. Exploration should create understanding rather
than reduce to simple completion.

## Social Meaning

Other players should feel like fellow inhabitants. Communities should be able
to form through shared goals, geography, relationships, and history, with
long-term social bonds valued over temporary interaction.

## Meaningful Change

Time and actions should matter within a continuous place. Change should
respect what came before, preserve coherence, and create possibilities for
what comes next.
