# Cosmology

Reality is participatory. To exist is to participate, and participation
inevitably transforms reality.

## Purpose

This document establishes the objective nature of reality in UMBRAL. It is not
mythology, religion, or speculation. It describes the conditions from which
history, life, identity, consciousness, and consequence must follow.

The document is written for developers. In-world accounts may be incomplete,
symbolic, or contradictory without changing the reality described here.

## Existence Is Fundamental

Existence is the starting condition. UMBRAL has no canonical creator, no
canonical first moment, and no canonical creation myth. The reason existence
exists remains intentionally unanswered.

UMBRAL begins from what must already be true if coherent reality exists:
something persists, changes occur, relationships form, and participation has
consequences. These conditions are not exceptions requiring further causes.

## One Reality, Two Aspects

Reality has two inseparable aspects: Manifest and Umbral.

Manifest describes immediate existence: matter, motion, sensation, structure,
and events as they are encountered directly. Umbral describes continuity,
consequence, identity, and persistent relationships: the ways participation
remains part of reality through change.

Manifest and Umbral are not dimensions, planes, worlds, or opposing realms.
Neither is more real than the other. They are distinct descriptions of one
reality. The Umbral is usually encountered through its effects rather than
directly perceived, which accounts for the incompleteness of all cultural
accounts of it.

## Continuity

Continuity is the persistence of identity through change. Identity does not
require identical matter. Continuity allows a participant to change without
being replaced by an unrelated participant.

Without continuity, identity, causality, history, and memory would be
impossible. Resonance is the persistence of meaningful continuity within
relationships created through participation; its behavior is defined in
[Resonance](RESONANCE.md) and is not repeated here.

## Participation

Participation is the continual formation, transformation, and dissolution of
relationships within reality.

Participation is not synonymous with deliberate action. Everything participates
simply by existing. A mountain participates in gravity, erosion, climate, and
the routes built around it. A river participates in a watershed, settlements,
trade, and the shaping of land. A person participates in families, language,
work, conflict, and memory. A city participates in economies, institutions,
and generations. A tool participates in the work it enables, the materials it
consumes, and the people who inherit its use. A forest participates in soil,
weather, animals, and the histories of those who depend upon it.

Participation is unavoidable. To exist already means to participate, and no
participation leaves reality exactly as it was.

## Relationship

Reality is fundamentally participatory because relationships are fundamental.
Nothing exists in isolation. Gravity, distance, family, language, trade,
conflict, and culture are all relationships through which participants affect
one another and acquire continuity.

Reality is not built from isolated objects. It is built from participants in
relationship. The significance of a participant therefore cannot be reduced
to its material composition or considered independently of its history.

## Resonance

Persistent relationships acquire Resonance. Resonance is the persistence of
significant relationships created through participation.

Resonance is universal, relational, emergent, and irreversible, but it is not
energy, matter, memory, information, magic, a resource, or an external force.
Its laws and limitations are defined in [Resonance](RESONANCE.md).

## Identity

Identity emerges from continuity. Identity is not matter, memory, or
information. Identity is the continuity of an organized participant across
change.

Repair preserves identity because it continues an existing organization.
Growth preserves identity because change can occur within continuity. Perfect
copying does not preserve identity because a copy begins a separate continuity,
even when its matter, memories, and information are identical.

## Life

Life is a pattern of continuity capable of actively maintaining and reproducing
itself through participation.

Life is not magical and is not a separate substance. It emerges naturally from
organized continuity that sustains itself through relationships with its
surroundings and with other participants.

## Consciousness

Consciousness is the lived experience of an integrated participant capable of
modeling both reality and itself.

Consciousness does not require a soul, detachable spirit, or supernatural
essence. It is an emergent property of integrated life: a way in which an
organized participant experiences and interprets its own participation.

Intention is participation guided by imagined possibility. An intentional act
therefore changes reality not because it is exempt from causality, but because
an integrated participant can represent possible consequences and act in
relation to them.

## Death

Death is the irreversible end of autonomous participation.

Death ends a living participant's capacity to maintain and direct its own
continuity. It does not erase the relationships formed during life. Those
relationships continue through other participants, Resonance remains, and
legacy persists as consequence within reality.

Death does not imply an afterlife. Reality contains consequences, and those
consequences may continue after autonomous participation has ended.

## Time

Time is the condition through which participation unfolds. It is not merely a
method of measurement.

Through time, relationships transform, continuity persists through change, and
history accumulates. Without time, relationships could not transform,
continuity could not exist, and history could not accumulate.

## Openness

Reality is never finished. Past participation constrains possibility, but it
never completely determines the future.

This openness is the Non-Determinism principle established in [Resonance](RESONANCE.md).
Resonance influences reality without dictating its outcomes. New participation
can therefore alter what follows while remaining accountable to what came
before.

## Closing

The universe is not merely old. It is experienced. Every participant leaves
reality different from how they found it.
