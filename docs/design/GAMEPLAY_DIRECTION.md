# Gameplay Direction

This document records the three project pillars that UMBRAL must satisfy
simultaneously. It is a gameplay-direction standard, not a specification of
final mechanics.

## Living World

A place that appears to possess history, relationships, routines,
consequences, and continuity independent of the player.

## Deep Rewarding Systems

Progression, gathering, crafting, combat, exploration, trade, equipment, social
systems, and long-term goals capable of sustaining play without reliance on
narrative consumption.

## Mechanical Excellence

Responsive controls, readable feedback, satisfying movement and interaction,
clear interfaces, strong audiovisual response, and reliable moment-to-moment
play.

If the first pillar succeeds alone, UMBRAL becomes an interesting setting rather
than a successful game.

If the second and third succeed without the first, UMBRAL becomes mechanically
competent but interchangeable.

The project requires all three.
